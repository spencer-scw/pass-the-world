Take turns playing one shared Terraria world and character with your friends. Each player gets a set amount of real time, then the world is passed to the next person.

Games are set up on the Pass the World website, https://passtheworld.skrewt.dev: sign in with Discord, create a game, and share the invite link. Your Discord channel can get an announcement whenever it's someone's turn.

In tModLoader, open the character select screen and click "Pass the World" to link your account. When it's your turn, click "Start turn" and you're loaded straight into the world. The mod:
- shows a countdown, with warnings before time runs out
- uploads checkpoints while you play, so a crash or a quit doesn't lose your turn
- saves, quits and hands the world on when your time is up
- keeps the shared world and character out of your normal world and character lists

Vanilla only for now: content mods aren't kept in sync between players.

Running your own server? Set the server address under Mod Configuration. Source and setup instructions: https://github.com/spencer-scw/pass-the-world
